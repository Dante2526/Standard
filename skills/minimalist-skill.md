# Skill: Premium minimalist & Editorial UI

## Aesthetic Foundation
- **Monochrome+**: Use a primary palette of whites, deep blacks (#111111), and a single warm accent (e.g., subtle cream or deep indigo).
- **Hard Grids**: Favor clean, flat "Bento" grids with minimal line-width borders.
- **Editorial Typography**: High-contrast type setting. Large, bold headings with generous letter spacing and tight line height.

## Design Restrictions
- **No Overused Fonts**: Absolutely no "Inter", "Roboto", or "Open Sans". Prefer modern grotesques or sophisticated serifs.
- **No Heavy Shadows**: Avoid generic Material Design shadows. Use thin, crisp border-strokes instead.
- **No Neon**: No aggressive gradients or glowing "cyberpunk" effects unless asked.

## Elements
- **Utility First**: UI elements should feel like precision tools.
- **Buttons**: Solid, minimalist blocks with micro-scaling on click.
- **Labels**: Small, caps, bold labels positioned outside of cards or containers to act as markers.
